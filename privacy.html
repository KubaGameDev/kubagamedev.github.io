Privacy.

JUMPKAT LTD handles information sent through this website. For questions or requests about your information, contact kuba@jumpkat.com.

When you contact us

We collect your name, email address, subject and message so we can read and respond to your enquiry. Please don't include passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information.

We use this information for our legitimate interest in handling business enquiries, or to take steps at your request before entering a contract where that applies. We don't add you to a marketing list through this form.

Storage and retention

Enquiries are stored in a private inbox on Amazon Web Services in the London region. Inbox messages expire after 90 days; removal can take a few extra days. If we continue the conversation by email or need to retain information for a contract or legal obligation, that correspondence may be kept separately for as long as needed.

Spam protection

The live form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check for automated abuse. Cloudflare processes technical information such as your IP address and browser information. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. Our backend uses a short-lived, keyed fingerprint of your IP address to limit submissions. It is not kept with your message and expires after two days.

Website services

AWS delivers the website and processes necessary technical request data. Google Fonts supplies typefaces and receives the technical information needed to deliver them. These providers may process information internationally under their applicable data-protection arrangements. This website does not use advertising trackers. Your colour-theme preference and arcade settings may be stored in your browser. The album link opens Spotify only when you choose it.

Your choices and rights

You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your information, or to object to our use of it. Other rights, including restriction and portability, may apply depending on the circumstances. You can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office.
