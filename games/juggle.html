Juggle Cats

Catch the cats as they fall. You have three lives.

Score: 0 · Lives: 3

Objective: Bounce falling cats before they reach the ground. Each catch earns a point; each missed cat costs a life.
Controls: When a cat is falling, click or tap it, press 1 / 2 / 3, or use the buttons. P pauses or resumes.
