Pong

A classic rally. First to 10 wins.

Choose a mode, then start a game.

You 0 : 0 Computer

Controls: W/S or ↑/↓ in solo mode. With two players, W/S moves the left paddle and ↑/↓ moves the right. Drag on the court to move a paddle; each player uses their half in two-player mode. Press P to pause.
